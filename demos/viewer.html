Eight bodies, one connectome

One fly circuit — the six leg motor pools of the ventral nerve cord and the interneurons that couple them — compiled once and bound to eight robots that already exist. Each robot is imported from its own published URDF, unmodified. Every joint you see move is driven by rate(agonist) − rate(antagonist) across a real antagonist muscle pair, resolved through the muscle names the connectome annotates.

What is measured, and what is not

The distinction is the point of the project, so it is on the page rather than in a footnote.

From the fly

  • Which neurons, and with what sign. Structure comes from the measurement; there is no training step.
  • Every joint command. Motor neurons are annotated by the muscle they innervate, so a joint is an antagonist pair and the command is the rate difference.
  • Which limb gets which circuit. Front, middle and hind leg pools bind to the robot limb that resolved to that segment.
  • The int8 arithmetic. Pruned at p(real)=0.95, quantised to 8 bits, ticked by the same reference runtime the emitted C is checked against.

Added for this demo

  • The rhythm. The unfitted circuit settles to a steady, leg-differentiated posture and does not oscillate. The step cycle is retarget.phase_oscillator, the Kuramoto ring the library ships for exactly this case, holding the retargeter's own gait phases.
  • The "go" signal. The legs circuit has no input port — the descending neurons that start walking live in a different circuit — so a tonic Q8.8 drive stands in for them.
  • Swing amplitude and within-leg phase. Scaled to each robot's own URDF joint limits, offset by the textbook quarter cycle. Absolute timing does not transfer from a fly leg to a robot leg.
  • Rotor mixing. Wing power and steering muscles to thrust and roll, for the quadrotor only.

Not simulated at all

  • Contact, gravity, mass, torque. Nothing here is a dynamics simulation; the robots are posed, not balanced.
  • ROS 2 itself. The packages are generated and complete, but rclpy is not a dependency of this repo and the node has never been spun.
  • Silicon. No hardware, and no measured power figure anywhere.
  • Meshes. Link geometry is the URDF's own primitives and joint frames; visual meshes were not fetched.
